Research

My research focuses on human-preference-aware autonomous driving. I study trajectory planning methods that incorporate human preferences toward normal-to-urgent driving scenarios.

ReaDy-Go: Real-to-Sim Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting Simulation for Environment-Specific Visual Navigation with Moving Obstacles
Yonjae Lim, Youngseok Jang, Dabin Kim, Youngsoo Han, Seungwoo Jung, H. Jin Kim
arXiv, 2026
project page / paper

ReaDy-Go generates photorealistic navigation datasets for dynamic environments by combining a reconstructed static GS scene with dynamic human GS obstacles, and trains policies robust to both the sim-to-real gap and moving obstacles.
